# Kestrel Robotics opens new assembly plant in Eindhoven

**EINDHOVEN, 9 February 2027** – Kestrel Robotics today announced the opening of its second European assembly plant, at Science Park 14 in Eindhoven. The 6,800 m² site will employ 220 people by the end of 2027 and triples the company's capacity for its K-7 warehouse robots.

"Eindhoven gives us the engineering talent we need to scale," said Sofia van Dijk, Chief Operating Officer of Kestrel Robotics.

The plant represents an investment of €48 million and is expected to ship its first units in April 2027.
